# Whiteboard

The architecture was first laid out on a shared Whiteboard, which holds three views in one place:

1. **Domain view: one cargo, many contracts.** Contracts attached to the cargo over time, the cargo's physical timeline, the compiler pipeline and the three P&L states.
2. **Logical architecture, A to Z.** The seven layers, the cross-cutting concerns, the six architecture invariants and the six agile increments.
3. **Cargo lifecycle map.** Nine phases left to right; physical events, contract events, six personas, holons and legacy infrastructure top to bottom; two threads followed through every layer.

Whiteboard (private until shared by its owner): https://claude.ai/artifact/Qd1y34vifXJsA5W1b9P1SL

Every view is also described in full, with diagrams, in the [white paper](white-paper.md).
